THE GAMES OF THE PIGEON GOD
Rulebook v1.5 — Decrees, Items and Rat abilities
Players: 2–4
Recommended Age: 14+
Estimated Playtime: 30 minutes
Genre: Tactical arena combat, public objectives, risk management, and elimination

1. OVERVIEW
In The Games of the Pigeon God, each player controls a Rat Gladiator fighting for survival, glory, and the favor of the almighty Pigeon God.
Players battle through 2 Arenas, earning Divine Favor by:
Winning Arenas.
Completing Decrees.
Reaching combat milestones.
Correctly betting on an Arena winner after being eliminated.
Resolving special card effects.
After the second Arena, the player with the most Divine Favor wins.

2. COMPONENTS
The game uses:
Modular hexagonal Arena board.
Rat Gladiator cards.
Rat meeples.
Cat meeple.
Decree cards.
Item cards.
Divine Favor tokens.
Health/Fervor tokens.
Player boards.
Attack trackers.
Dodge trackers.
Finish trackers.
Action markers.
Six-sided dice.
Terrain elements, including:
Rocks.
Large crates.
Sewers.
Rat Burrows.

3. DIVINE FAVOR
Divine Favor represents prestige earned before the Pigeon God.
It is the main victory resource.
Divine Favor:
Is never reset during the Match.
Carries from Arena 1 into Arena 2.
Determines the winner of the game.

4. RAT GLADIATORS
Each player uses a different Rat during each Arena.
Every Rat has:
Health
Attack
Speed
A special ability, when applicable
Attack
Attack determines how many dice that individual Rat rolls when attacking.
Attack 2 means roll exactly 2 Attack dice; Attack 4 means roll exactly 4. There is no universal three-dice rule for Rats.
In Arena 1, every roll of 4, 5 or 6 is a successful Attack worth 1 Hit. A 4 is a Hit. The Cat always uses its own 3 Attack dice.
Speed
Speed determines:
How many hexes the Rat may move with one Move Action.
How many dice the Rat rolls when Dodging.
A Rat may move up to its Speed.
It is never required to use all available movement.
Health
Health represents how much damage a Rat can take.
When a Rat reaches 0 Health, it is Finished and removed from the Arena.

5. RAT DRAFT
At the beginning of the Match, each player receives 3 Rat cards.
Each player:
Secretly chooses 1 Rat to keep.
Passes the other 2 cards to the player on their right.
Receives 2 cards from the player on their left.
Chooses 1 of those Rats to keep.
Discards the remaining Rat.
Each player therefore finishes the draft with 2 secret Rats.
Before Arena 1, each player secretly chooses which of those Rats will fight first.
The other Rat is reserved for Arena 2.
A Rat remains hidden from opponents until it enters play.

6. STANDARD ARENA SETUP
With 2 players, build a 19-hex radius-2 Arena. Each player selects an exterior Burrow adjacent to its perimeter. Exterior Burrows do not count toward the 19 Arena hexes.
With 3 or 4 players, build a 37-hex radius-3 Arena. Each player selects a Burrow on an outermost-ring Arena hex. These Burrow hexes are included in the 37.
Mark the center as the Cat spawn. Place two connected Sewers, one rock and one large crate. Locations may be randomized. The digital generator uses seeded radius-2 terrain locations, preserving clear center neighbors and connectivity.
Determine Turn order before Burrow placement. Starting with the first player and continuing in Turn order, each player chooses a distinct legal Burrow location. No Turns or Cat rolls begin until everyone has placed.
A Burrow must have at least one adjacent normal Arena entrance. Rock and crate cannot block all its entrances. One entrance may be blocked if another is clear. Burrows cannot occupy terrain obstacles, Sewers or another Burrow; placement cannot seal a previously placed Burrow's final entrance.
For exterior Burrows, legal locations are grid-aligned hexes directly adjacent to the perimeter. A Rat may use any legal adjacent entrance, rather than one fixed assigned entrance.
Each Rat begins in its own Burrow. Burrows remain one-way for both board sizes: after departure, Rats cannot enter, cross or be pushed into a Burrow. The Cat's ordinary movement treats a Burrow as blocked.
Leaving costs one hex of movement; the Rat may use the remaining Speed in the same Move.
A Rat must attempt departure on its first Turn and cannot voluntarily End Turn while in its Burrow. The same requirement applies on later Turns if it has not yet left.
Keep the Cat roll at the beginning of the Turn. If the Cat or another Rat occupies an entrance, the departing Rat may enter that hex to initiate combat, requiring and consuming both Actions as usual. If the Rat loses or ties that direct attack, it returns one hex to its own Burrow and its Turn ends; it must retry departure next Turn. This is the explicit exception to mandatory departure and one-way re-entry.
Direct entrance combat against either a Rat or the Cat uses the same retreat-and-retry exception. This supersedes the earlier empty-entry restriction.
Shuffle the Decree deck and reveal four public Decrees. Shuffle the Item deck.
Repeat Burrow selection on each new Arena layout, in that Arena's Turn order. Final Duels and restarted duels use the same setup according to the number of duelists.

7. ARENA STRUCTURE
Each Arena lasts a maximum of:
5 Rounds.
During every Round, each participating player receives one Turn.
A living Rat normally has:
2 Actions per Turn.
An Arena may end before Round 5 if only one Rat remains alive.

8. TURN STRUCTURE
A living player's Turn follows this sequence:
Move the Cat.
Resolve any Cat combat.
Gain Arena-specific start-of-Turn effects.
Receive 2 Actions.
Spend Actions.
Resolve any combats immediately when triggered.
Resolve completed Decrees.
End Turn. A living Rat cannot voluntarily end its Turn in a Burrow; the failed direct Cat attack exception in section 6 still ends the Turn.
An eliminated player still receives a Turn, but only to move the Cat.

9. ACTIONS
The main Actions are:
Move
Ask the Crowd for an Item
Actions may be repeated during the same Turn as long as they remain legal.
There is no separate basic Attack Action.
Combat is triggered automatically when a Rat attempts to enter an occupied hex, and requires and consumes both Actions.

10. MOVEMENT
A Move Action allows a Rat to move up to its Speed in hexes.
The Rat may stop early.
A Rat cannot move through:
Rocks.
Large crates.
Blocking terrain.
Another Rat.
The Cat.
If the moving Rat attempts to enter a hex occupied by another Rat or the Cat, movement pauses and combat begins immediately.
A Move that enters a Rat or Cat hex requires and consumes both Actions. Resolve combat and displacement, then automatically end the Rat's Turn. No unused movement can be continued. With only one Action remaining, combat cannot be initiated.

11. HEX OCCUPANCY
Two Rats may never finish a Turn on the same hex.
Entering another Rat's hex triggers combat immediately.
The same principle applies when entering the Cat's hex.
Combat determines which figure keeps the contested position.

12. RAT VS. RAT COMBAT
The Rat entering the occupied hex is the Attacker.
The Rat already occupying the hex is the Defender.
Combat proceeds as follows:
Attacker rolls Attack dice.
Attacker may spend Fervor to reroll dice.
Attacker confirms the Attack roll.
Defender rolls Dodge dice.
Defender may spend Fervor to reroll dice.
Defender confirms the Dodge roll.
Resolve Hits.
Resolve Dodges.
Resolve Counterattacks.
Apply damage simultaneously.
Check for Finishes.
Determine combat winner.
Resolve Pushback.
The Defender does not make a normal attack.
The Defender may only deal damage through Counterattacks.

13. ATTACK ROLLS — ARENA 1
For each Attack die:
1–3: Miss
4–6: 1 Hit
Each uncancelled Hit deals:
1 damage.
Every point of damage successfully dealt increases the player's Attack Tracker by 1.

14. DODGING
The Defender rolls a number of Dodge dice equal to their Speed.
For each Dodge die:
1–4: Failure
5: Cancel 1 Hit
6: Cancel 1 Hit and deal 1 Counterattack damage
Every result of 5 or 6 increases the player's Dodge Tracker by 1.

15. COUNTERATTACKS
Each natural 6 rolled while Dodging:
Cancels 1 incoming Hit.
Deals 1 damage to the Attacker.
Counterattack damage counts as successful damage and therefore also increases the player's Attack Tracker.
Multiple 6s may cause multiple Counterattack damage.

16. COMBAT EXAMPLE
The Attacker scores:
3 Hits
The Defender rolls:
2, 5, 6
The Defender therefore:
Cancels 2 Hits.
Receives 1 damage.
Deals 1 Counterattack damage.
Gains +2 on the Dodge Tracker.
Gains +1 on the Attack Tracker for the Counterattack damage.
Both Rats suffer 1 damage.
Because the combat damage is tied, the Defender wins the combat.

17. WINNING A COMBAT
Compare the amount of damage dealt by each Rat during that combat.
Attacker dealt more damage
The Attacker wins.
The Defender is pushed away.
Defender dealt more damage
The Defender wins.
The Attacker returns to the hex from which it entered.
Equal damage
The Defender wins.
The Attacker retreats.
This includes a 0–0 result.
The Attacker must therefore deal strictly more damage than the Defender to capture the occupied hex.

18. PUSHBACK
When the Defender loses, the combat winner chooses an adjacent legal and empty hex for the losing Rat.
The destination must:
Be inside the Arena.
Not contain blocking terrain.
Not contain another Rat.
Not contain the Cat.
If the losing defender has no legal adjacent empty hex, the living Rats switch positions: the attacker captures the contested hex and the defender takes the hex the attacker occupied immediately before combat. The winning attacker does not retreat.

19. COMBAT ENDS THE TURN
Entering a Rat or Cat hex costs both Actions. Once rolls, damage, Finishes and displacement are resolved, the Turn ends automatically. No additional Move or remaining-Speed continuation is allowed.
If the attacker loses or ties, it retreats ONE HEX along its approach path to the hex occupied immediately before entering combat. It does not retrace the entire Move.
If the Arena has ended, do not start another Turn.

20. FINISHES
When a Rat reaches 0 Health:
It is immediately removed from the Arena.
The character responsible for the final damage gains 1 Finish.
Finish milestones:
1 Finish: No bonus.
2 Finishes: +1 Fervor.
3 Finishes: +1 Divine Favor.
Trackers reset when a new Arena begins.

21. SIMULTANEOUS DEATH
Attack and Counterattack damage are resolved as part of the same combat resolution.
It is therefore possible for both Rats to reach 0 Health.
If this occurs:
Both Rats are removed.
Each gains the appropriate Finish for eliminating the other.
If they were the last surviving Rats:
the Arena ends immediately with no winner.

22. ATTACK TRACKER
The Attack Tracker records damage successfully dealt, not Attack Actions.
Each damage dealt gives:
+1 Attack
This includes:
Normal Attack damage.
Counterattack damage.
Milestones:
4 Attacks: +1 Fervor
5 Attacks: +1 Fervor
Rewards are gained immediately.

23. DODGE TRACKER
Every successful Dodge result increases the Dodge Tracker by 1.
Therefore:
A 5 gives +1 Dodge.
A 6 gives +1 Dodge and may also cause Counterattack damage.
Milestones:
4 Dodges: +1 Fervor
5 Dodges: +1 Fervor
Rewards are gained immediately.

24. FERVOR
Fervor represents a Rat's growing battle frenzy.
Fervor may be gained through:
Losing Health.
Attack milestones.
Dodge milestones.
Finish milestones.
Special abilities.
Items.
Arena effects.
Whenever a Rat loses Health, the corresponding Health token may be flipped to its Fervor side.
Spending Fervor
Spend:
1 Fervor
to reroll:
1 Attack or Dodge die.
The same die may be rerolled multiple times as long as the player continues spending Fervor.
However, roll phases are final.
Once the Attacker confirms the Attack roll, those dice can no longer be changed.
The Dodge phase then begins.
Once the Defender confirms the Dodge roll, combat is resolved.

25. THE CAT
The Cat is a neutral creature roaming the Arena.
The Cat has:
9 Health
and attacks with:
3 Attack Dice.
The Cat:
Cannot Dodge.
Can take Counterattack damage.
Can be Finished by a Rat.
Retains its remaining Health between Arenas if it survives.

26. CAT MOVEMENT
At the beginning of every player's Turn:
That player rolls 1 six-sided die.
Each number corresponds to one of the six hex directions.
The Cat moves exactly 1 hex in that direction.
The Cat therefore moves once per player Turn.

27. CAT LEAVING THE ARENA
If the Cat is on an edge hex and its movement would take it outside the Arena:
the Cat immediately respawns at its spawn hex.
In the standard Arena, the spawn hex is the center.

28. CAT VS. RAT COMBAT
If the Cat moves into a Rat's hex:
The Cat becomes the Attacker.
The Cat rolls its 3 Attack dice.
Hits are resolved normally.
The Rat rolls its normal Dodge dice.
Dodge results of 6 may Counterattack and damage the Cat.
If a Rat voluntarily moves into the Cat's hex:
the Rat becomes the Attacker.
Because the Cat cannot Dodge, the Rat's Attack Hits deal damage directly.

29. CAT COMBAT WINNER
When the Cat attacks a Rat, compare damage dealt during the combat just as in normal Rat combat.
If the Cat wins:
the Rat is pushed one hex along the direction in which the Cat entered.
If that hex is illegal:
the Cat retreats instead.
If the Rat deals more damage through Counterattacks:
the Rat wins and chooses a legal adjacent hex to push the Cat back to. A legal previous Cat hex may be chosen.
A tied combat favors the Defender, as normal.

30. FINISHING THE CAT
If a Rat reduces the Cat to 0 Health:
that Rat gains:
+1 Finish
The Cat immediately respawns at its spawn hex.
When the Cat respawns after being Finished, it returns with:
9 Health.
If the Cat survives an Arena without being Finished, its remaining Health carries into the next Arena.
Example:
Cat ends Arena 1 with 4 Health.
Cat begins Arena 2 with 4 Health.

31. ELIMINATED PLAYERS
When a Rat is Finished:
It is removed from the Arena.
Its player no longer receives normal Actions.
Its player continues participating through the Cat.
On that player's Turn:
Roll the Cat movement die.
Resolve Cat movement.
Resolve Cat combat if necessary.
End the Turn.
The Cat does not receive any extra movement because the player was eliminated.

32. CAT FINISH REWARD FOR ELIMINATED PLAYERS
If an eliminated player's Cat movement directly causes the Cat to Finish another Rat:
the eliminated player gains:
+1 Divine Favor.

33. BETTING AFTER ELIMINATION
A player Finished during:
Round 1,
Round 2, or
Round 3
may publicly bet on which surviving Rat will win the Arena.
The player places their marker at that player's Burrow or designated betting area.
If the prediction is correct:
gain +1 Divine Favor.
Players eliminated during Rounds 4 or 5 cannot place a bet.

34. SEWERS
The standard Arena contains 2 connected Sewers.
When a Rat enters one Sewer:
It immediately teleports to the other Sewer.
It must immediately exit onto a legal adjacent hex.
It may never finish movement on the Sewer itself.
Teleporting and exiting:
Cost no additional Action.
Consume no additional movement.
Only the movement used to enter the Sewer counts against Speed.
Example:
A Speed 3 Rat spends 1 movement to enter a Sewer.
It teleports and exits for free.
It still has 2 movement remaining.

35. DECREES OF THE PIGEON GOD
Decrees are:
Public.
Shared.
Available to all players.
There are always up to:
4 active Decrees
visible in the Arena.
When a player completes one:
They immediately claim it.
They receive its reward.
The Decree is discarded.
A replacement Decree is revealed.
Only one player may claim each Decree.

36. IMMEDIATE DECREES
Some Decrees resolve immediately when their condition is reached.
Examples include:
Reach a certain number of Attacks.
Reach a certain number of Dodges.
Reach a certain number of Finishes.
The first player to meet the requirement claims the Decree.
The replacement becomes active immediately.

37. END-OF-ARENA DECREES
Some Decrees are evaluated only when the Arena ends.
Examples include:
Finish with the most Attacks.
Finish with the most remaining Health.
Finish with exactly 1 Health.
Occupy a particular location.
For comparative Decrees, a player must strictly outperform every other eligible player.
If two or more players tie for the best result:
nobody claims the Decree.
No secondary tiebreaker is used.

38. ITEMS
Items provide temporary benefits.
A Rat may have a maximum of:
1 equipped Item.

39. ASKING THE CROWD FOR AN ITEM
A player may spend:
1 Action
to ask the crowd for an Item if:
They do not already have an Item.
They are not currently sharing a hex because of an unresolved Rat combat.
They are not currently sharing a hex because of an unresolved Cat combat.
The player then:
Draws exactly 1 Item card.
Must keep it.
Equips it immediately.

40. USING ITEMS
Using an Item normally costs:
0 Actions
unless the card specifically states otherwise.
Each Item card specifies when it may be used.
Items are generally used during their owner's Turn, unless the card explicitly creates another timing window.
When a consumable Item is used:
discard it.
All unused Items are discarded at the end of the Arena.

41. EARLY END OF AN ARENA
If only 1 Rat remains alive at any moment:
the Arena ends immediately.
That Rat automatically wins the Arena.
If all remaining Rats are Finished simultaneously and nobody survives:
the Arena ends with no winner.

42. NORMAL END OF AN ARENA
If two or more Rats remain alive after Round 5, determine the winner among living Rats only.
Compare, in order:
Most Finishes.
Most Attacks.
Most Dodges.
Most remaining Health.
The first criterion that breaks the tie determines the Arena winner. If all four criteria remain tied for first place, the Arena has no winner and awards no Arena victory Divine Favor.
The Arena winner receives:
+2 Divine Favor.

43. BETWEEN ARENAS
After Arena 1:
Keep:
Divine Favor.
The Cat's current Health.
Discard or reset:
Arena 1 Rat.
Items.
Fervor.
Attack Tracker.
Dodge Tracker.
Finish Tracker.
Elimination status.
Bet.
Remaining Actions.
Each player then reveals and deploys their second drafted Rat for Arena 2.

44. FIRST PLAYER
Arena 1
Determine the first player randomly.
Play then continues clockwise.
Arena 2
The player with the least Divine Favor becomes first player.
If multiple players are tied for least Divine Favor:
the tied player who acted latest in the previous Arena's Turn order becomes first player.
Play then continues clockwise.

45. ARENA 2 — THE FINAL ARENA
Arena 2 uses all normal rules, but adds two global special rules.
These represent the Pigeon God's increasing desire for violence and spectacle.

46. FAVOR OF THE CENTER
At the beginning of a living player's Turn:
if their Rat currently occupies the Arena's center hex:
gain +1 Fervor.
A Rat may gain this reward once each Round if it manages to maintain or recapture the center.

47. BRUTAL ATTACKS
During Arena 2, Attack dice become more dangerous.
For Rats and the Cat:
1–3: Miss
4: 1 Hit
5: 1 Hit
6: 2 Hits
Dodge rules remain unchanged.
Therefore, a Dodge result of 6 still:
Cancels only 1 Hit.
Causes 1 Counterattack damage.

48. END OF THE MATCH
After Arena 2 ends:
compare every player's total Divine Favor.
The player with the most Divine Favor wins The Games of the Pigeon God.

49. FINAL TIE — THE PIGEON GOD'S DUEL
If two or more players are tied for the highest Divine Favor:
they enter a Final Duel.
Each tied player chooses either of the 2 Rats they used during the Match.
All selected Rats place Burrows in Turn order on a new Final Duel layout: 19 hexes with exterior Burrows for two duelists, or 37 hexes with outer-ring Burrows for three or four. Each starts at its full printed Health. Reset Attack, Dodge and Finish trackers, Fervor, elimination state, bets and Actions. Divine Favor remains unchanged.
The Duel continues until only:
1 Rat remains alive.
That player's Rat wins the Match.
During the Final Duel:
No Decrees are active.
No betting occurs.
No new Items are drawn.
The Cat is not used.
No Divine Favor is awarded.
Use Arena 1 Attack dice rules: 4, 5 and 6 each cause one Hit. No Arena 2 center Fervor applies. Combat still requires both Actions and ends the Turn after displacement. There is no five-Round limit; keep playing until one Rat survives. Eliminated duelists are skipped.
The last living Rat wins. If the last living Rats die simultaneously and none remain, restart the Final Duel at full Health with the same chosen Rats. No Divine Favor is awarded, including Finish milestones.

53. GOLDEN RULE
Whenever a card or Rat ability directly contradicts a general rule:
the specific card or ability takes precedence.

54. CAT CLARIFICATIONS FOR THE DIGITAL GAME
At the beginning of every Turn, roll Cat movement once before granting a living player Actions. Cat-initiated combat at this point does not consume a living player's future Actions: resolve it first, then grant two Actions if that player survives. An eliminated player ends their Turn after this single Cat movement/combat sequence.
A Cat movement blocked by a rock or large crate consumes the roll but leaves the Cat in place. Cat movement does not use Rat Sewer teleportation.
Leaving the Arena respawns the Cat at its spawn while preserving remaining Health. Being Finished respawns it at 9 Health and grants the responsible Rat one Finish. Surviving Health, including post-respawn Health, carries into the next Arena.
If the respawn hex is occupied by a Rat, resolve a Cat attack against that Rat. The Cat rolls three Attack dice and the Rat Dodges using Speed. The Cat has no prior approach direction when respawning: if the Cat wins, the active player chooses a legal adjacent pushback hex for the Rat; if the Cat loses or ties, the winning Rat's player chooses a legal adjacent hex for the Cat. The Cat may not overlap the Rat after resolution.
When the Cat enters a Rat by ordinary movement and wins, push the Rat one hex in the Cat's movement direction. If that forward destination is illegal, the Cat retreats instead. If the Cat loses or ties, the winning Rat's player chooses legal Cat pushback.
A Rat voluntarily entering the Cat's hex requires and spends both Actions. Its Attack stat determines its dice. The Cat never rolls Dodge dice. If the Rat wins and the Cat survives, the Rat's player chooses legal Cat pushback; if no legal hex exists, Rat and Cat swap positions. If the Rat loses or ties, it retreats one approach hex. This Rat-initiated combat ends the Rat's Turn after resolution, including any immediate respawn combat.
Only a player who was already eliminated when rolling Cat movement gains one Divine Favor if that movement/combat causes the Cat to Finish another Rat. A living roller receives no such reward.

55. DECREE REFERENCE FILTER
The supplied Decree grid is reference content for the next content milestone. Exclude Decrees requiring separately tracked Dodges against the Cat, including Burla al gato. General Dodge Decrees remain eligible. Doma al gato, which rewards defeating the Cat, is retained. Do not add a separate Cat-Dodge tracker.


56. CURRENT CARD CONTENT AND OWNER CLARIFICATIONS
The supplied Rat, Item and Decree grids define current content, subject to the exclusions and clarifications here. All cards mentioning Oro are excluded entirely: Ratón Mercader and Ratón del Gato are not playable. Oro is not Divine Favor and must not be introduced as a resource. Burla al gato is excluded as previously specified. The active catalogs contain 18 Rats, 17 Items and 19 Decrees.
A card-granted Action or movement overrides normal combat Turn completion. Resolve damage and ordinary pushback first, then surviving participants resolve granted movement and Actions. Arena or Match termination takes precedence when at most one Rat remains. One additional Action cannot initiate combat, which still requires two available Actions.
Abilities limited to once per Turn reset each Turn, not each combat. Extra Actions earned outside a player's own Turn are resolved immediately after combat as a Move or Request Item opportunity, without another Cat roll or changing normal Turn order.
Dados gemelos means any matching pair in the final Attack dice, including two 1s, two 2s or two 3s. Ratón Místico replaces the ordinary incoming Hits with three incoming damage, which Dodge cancels normally; counterattacks still apply. It does not bypass Dodge.
Die-increasing abilities are optional and affect one chosen die once per applicable roll, capped at six. A later reroll replaces that die's result. For movement-sensitive abilities, movement completed before entering the contested hex counts; the entry that triggers combat itself is not a prior movement step. Ratón Esquivo triggers once per defending combat with at least one successful Dodge die (5 or 6), granting up to two hexes after displacement.
Chile is a consumable token worth one normal Fervor. A living player can consume it during play when they want an extra Fervor token; that Fervor does not expire at Turn end. Normal Arena-end Fervor reset still applies.
Clavo torcido adds one extra legal adjacent push after ordinary combat pushback when the Attack caused at least one damage. If the target survives and a legal destination exists, select that extra push destination; if none exists, no extra push is possible.
Generic Finish objectives include Cat Finishes. Pacifista del palomo requires survival with zero damage dealt during the Arena, including counterattack damage; missed attack attempts do not disqualify it. Successful Attacks on cards use actual damage dealt. Desafía al gato uses damage dealt to the Cat, including counters. No separate Cat-Dodge tracker exists.
Turno 5 on cards means Round 5. Conditions explicitly requiring Round 5 do not award when an Arena ends in an earlier Round. Comparative Decrees require strict superiority among living eligible Rats; a tie awards nobody. Noncomparative simultaneous eligibility uses active-player-first Turn order.
Four public Decrees remain active while enough cards exist. Completion awards once, discards the claimed card and immediately reveals its replacement. Newly active conditions are evaluated immediately. Decks and unclaimed Decrees carry between Arenas; used and unused equipped Items are discarded, and per-Arena card trackers reset. One physical copy of each active supplied card is used in the prototype deck; exhausted decks are not reshuffled.
Each normal Rat can hold one Item. Ratón Ingeniero can hold two; each request still costs one Action and draws exactly one mandatory Item. Requests are unavailable while still in a Burrow, preventing expenditure of the mandatory exit Actions. Consuming an Item costs zero Actions. Before-roll Items require the explicit pre-roll window. Arena en los ojos is the explicit exception allowing a defender to force one successful Attack die reroll after attacker confirmation, before Dodge.
Tapa de botella cancels one incoming Hit. Escudo de corcholata cancels incoming attack damage; neither cancels rolled Dodge counters. Brasa adds one damage to the next actual counterattack, once, rather than once per six. Red cannot reduce Dodge dice below zero.
Sardina chooses the Cat direction instead of rolling. Cascabel and Moneda can reroll an actually rolled Cat direction before movement; a chosen Sardina direction is not a roll. Silbato moves the Cat to an adjacent empty hex before its ordinary movement. Migajas adds a legal adjacent movement after the ordinary movement/combat sequence and may initiate Cat combat; it does not add another random movement roll.
Patines adds two movement to one Move. Hueso pulido adds up to one hex after a completed Move. Voluntary bonus movement shares the normal empty-hex and Sewer rules, consumes no Actions, and cannot initiate combat without the required two Actions. No movement may end on a Sewer. Forced pushback remains an adjacent empty non-Sewer displacement.
Final Duels retain Rat combat abilities but disable Items, Decrees and Favor rewards. All Items and card counters reset before a duel or restarted duel.
