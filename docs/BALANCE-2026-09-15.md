## Findings for owner review

- Winning Divine Favor averages **8.962**, ranging from **2 to 24**. Final Duels occur in **6.9%** of Matches.
- The Cat causes **1.201 Rat Finishes per Match**. Compare this with future human data before deciding whether it is too influential.
- **706 of 1,652 bets** succeed (42.7%).
- **Tame the Cat** is claimed in **1 of 401 exposures**. Inspect opportunity and AI incentives before changing its reward.
- **More Blood** (358/365) and **Elusive** (369/372) are claimed very frequently. Exposure timing and the current strategy affect these rates; review reward versus effort.
- Cat-control Item use is low: Whistle 13/290, Crumbs 10/308, Sardine 11/277, Jingle Bell 0/271. Inspect legal timing and AI choice behavior first. Zero use is not evidence that a card has no strategic value.
- Ember is used 275/275 times, Chili 271/272 and Shiny Coin 294/300. Usage frequency alone does not establish power.
- No Rat crosses the exploratory threshold of eight percentage points above/below the pooled Match-win association with at least 50 appearances. Pooling can hide player-count and Arena differences; this is not proof of balance.

Compare Rat appearances separately by Arena and player count before attributing an Arena 2 advantage. Human data is not yet sufficient for a human-versus-simulation balance conclusion. The collector and private export retain the necessary controller/segment/version fields for later comparisons.
